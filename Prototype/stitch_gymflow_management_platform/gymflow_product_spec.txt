# GymFlow Product Specification

GymFlow is a premium B2B SaaS platform for gym management, targeting owners, coaches, and staff. The design aesthetic is heavily inspired by Linear, Stripe, and Vercel—modern, minimal, dark-mode first, and highly professional.

## Target Audience
- Gym Owners (Business oversight)
- Admins (Staff and operation management)
- Coaches (Client management and workout programming)

## Design Principles
- **Minimalist & Premium**: No gym-bro aesthetics. Clean, data-driven, and elegant.
- **Dark Mode First**: Primary background #090B0F.
- **High Readability**: Inter typography, generous spacing, clear hierarchy.
- **Action-Oriented**: Focus on "What is happening?", "What needs attention?", and "What's next?".

## Planned Screens

1.  **Auth Flow** (Login, Register, Forgot Password)
    - Modern split-screen layout.
2.  **Dashboard**
    - High-level overview: Stats, Today's Sessions, Quick Actions, Activity Feed.
3.  **Exercises & Workouts** (Exercises, Workouts, Workout Sessions, Workout Logs)
    - Database management and workout builder.
4.  **Management** (Members, Coaches, Workout Assignments)
    - CRM-style tables and profiles.
5.  **Analytics & System** (Reports, Notifications, Profile, Settings)
    - Data visualization and configuration.
