10. FAQ Accordion Module

· ORGANISM // ACCORDION-SET
INTERACTIVE
FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Technical Specifications FAQ

How difficult is the hardware turnstile installation? ▼

Our hardware controller connects directly via standard Wiegand or OSDP v2 interfaces into your existing turnstiles, optical speed gates, or mag-locks. On average, facility technicians complete physical wiring in under 45 minutes.

How does STREN migrate our existing member database? ▼

We provide one-click migration bridges for Mindbody, ABC Fitness, Mariana Tek, and custom CSV databases. Member billing vaults and active waivers transfer automatically with zero downtime.

Can our turnstiles operate if the gym’s local internet disconnects? ▼

Yes. The STREN Edge Relay hardware stores encrypted local credentials for all active members. During an internet blackout, turnstiles continue verifying passes locally in 0.18s and sync timestamps once reconnected.

How does the AI periodization engine handle injured athletes? ▼

Trainers or athletes can flag specific joint limitations or pain points. The algorithmic engine automatically substitutes exercises preserving equivalent biomechanical stimulus while bypassing contraindicated angles.

Is there a cap on the number of staff or trainers we can add? ▼

Starter includes 5 staff accounts, Professional supports 25 certified trainers, and Enterprise allows unlimited staff seats with custom role-based permission tiers (e.g. Front Desk, Coach, Accounting).

What compliance certifications does STREN carry for card data? ▼

All payment details are handled via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant tokenized vaults. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II certified with end-to-end 256-bit encryption on both telemetry streams and member profiles.
